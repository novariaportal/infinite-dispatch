Infinite Dispatch User Guide

Welcome to Infinite Dispatch. This guide explains the core gameplay loop, account setup, and how to use the dashboard.

1) Create Your Account

  1. Enter your email + password.
  2. Choose a Base Airport (ICAO) at signup.
  3. Click Login / Register.

Important: Your base airport is locked after signup.

2) Base Airport Rules

  • You are always stationed at your selected base.
  • Routes will return to base within 3 legs.
  • Your employer is assigned from airlines that fit your base, and jobs stay tied to that airline.

3) Job Market Slots

The job board shows a fixed number of slots based on total hours:

  • 0–150: 2
  • 150–350: 4
  • 350–550: 5
  • 550–650: 7
  • 650–900: 8
  • 900+: 9–18

4) License & Rank Progression

  • PPL / FO → 1.0× pay
  • CPL / SFO → 1.5× pay
  • MPL / CPT → 2.0× pay
  • ATPL / SR CPT → 2.5× pay

5) Choose Your Server

Select the server you are flying on (Casual, Training, or Expert) before dispatching your flight.

The tracker will use your server selection to find your live flight data.

6) SimBrief Integration

  1. Enter your SimBrief username.
  2. Click Fetch Plan.
  3. The plan is saved automatically in Supabase.

7) Flight Tracking

Click Start Flight & Tracking after fetching a plan.

The tracking session is saved in Supabase and marked as EN ROUTE.

Live position updates are pulled by a Supabase Edge Function every few minutes.

Tracking now shows your Callsign and an in-app ETA estimate while en route.

Pay and hours are awarded only after completion is validated near destination (not just by disconnect).

8) Tracking History

The dashboard shows your latest Infinite Dispatch flights (server, status, and route).

9) Password Reset

Use the Forgot Password link on the login screen to request a reset email.

10) Data Storage

All accounts, profiles, SimBrief plans, and tracking sessions are stored in Supabase.
