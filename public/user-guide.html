Infinite Dispatch User Guide

Welcome to Infinite Dispatch. This guide explains the core gameplay loop, account setup, and how to use the dashboard.

1) Create Your Account

  1. Enter your email + password.
  2. Choose a Base Airport (ICAO) at signup.
  3. Click Login / Register.

Important: Your base airport is locked after signup.

2) Base Airport Rules

  • You are always stationed at your selected base.
  • Routes will return to base within 3 legs.
  • Airline assignments are random but must operate from your base.

3) Job Market Slots

The job board shows a fixed number of slots based on total hours:

  • 0–150: 2
  • 150–350: 4
  • 350–550: 5
  • 550–650: 7
  • 650–900: 8
  • 900+: 9–18

4) License & Rank Progression

  • PPL / FO → 1.0× pay
  • CPL / SFO → 1.5× pay
  • MPL / CPT → 2.0× pay
  • ATPL / SR CPT → 2.5× pay

5) SimBrief Integration

  1. Enter your SimBrief username.
  2. Click Fetch Plan.
  3. The plan is saved automatically in Supabase.

6) Flight Tracking

Click Start Flight & Tracking after fetching a plan.

The tracking session is saved in Supabase and marked as EN ROUTE.

Live position tracking will be enabled in the next backend update.

7) Password Reset

Use the Forgot Password link on the login screen to request a reset email.

8) Data Storage

All accounts, profiles, SimBrief plans, and tracking sessions are stored in Supabase.
