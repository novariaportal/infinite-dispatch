How to Run a Full Dispatch Workflow in Infinite Flight

Use this guide to turn accepted jobs into tracked, validated flights with a repeatable process.

1) Accept jobs that match your ratings

Open Job Market and choose a route that fits your current aircraft type ratings.

2) Generate your dispatch route

In Dispatch Center, generate a 2–3 leg route that returns toward your base while respecting aircraft range constraints.

3) Optional: import SimBrief

Fetch your latest SimBrief plan to keep route planning aligned with your preflight setup.

4) Start tracking

Start a tracking session from Dispatch Center to capture callsign, route, status, and ETA in your history timeline.

5) Complete and progress

Validated completions feed your progression with additional hours, economy growth, and better job opportunities.
